All names and terms are fictional. This fixture is released under the repository's MIT license.

1. Parties
This agreement is between Northstar Studio (the Customer) and Harbor Analytics (the Supplier).

2. Effective Date and Term
The agreement starts on 1 January 2026 and remains effective for twelve months.

3. Services
The Supplier will provide monthly data quality reports and maintain the Customer's analytics dashboard.

4. Fees and Payment
The monthly fee is EUR 2,000, excluding VAT. Invoices are payable within 30 days of receipt.

5. Termination
Either party may terminate the agreement with 60 days of written notice. A material breach permits termination after a 15-day cure period.

6. Confidentiality
Each party must protect confidential information for three years after termination. Information already public is excluded from this obligation.

7. Data Protection
The Supplier may process personal data only on the Customer's documented instructions. The Supplier must notify the Customer of a personal data breach within 24 hours of discovery.

8. Liability
Aggregate liability is capped at the fees paid during the preceding twelve months. The cap does not apply to fraud or deliberate misconduct.

9. Governing Law
The agreement is governed by the laws of Italy. Disputes will be submitted to the courts of Venice.

10. Intellectual Property
The Customer owns the final reports. The Supplier retains ownership of its pre-existing tools and libraries.
